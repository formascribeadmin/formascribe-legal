# Privacy Policy for Formascribe

**Effective Date:** November 21, 2026

Formascribe ("we," "our," or "us") is built as an offline-first, privacy-respecting companion for daily sketching. Your creative practice stays entirely on your device.

## 1. Information We Do Not Collect
Formascribe **does not** collect, transmit, store on external servers, or sell any personal data, usage analytics, advertising identifiers, or device telemetry. You do not need to create an account or log in to use the app.

## 2. Local On-Device Storage
All app data—including your completed sketch dates, streak history, prompt preferences (likes/dislikes), and notification settings—is stored locally on your device using Android's local storage (DataStore).
* This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly use Android's standard system backup or device-to-device transfer features, or choose to export/share a visual prompt card using Android's system share sheet.
* Uninstalling the app permanently deletes its local app data from your device.

## 3. Device Permissions
Formascribe requests only the minimum permissions necessary for optional features:
* **Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`):** Used solely to deliver optional daily sketching reminders scheduled locally on your device. You can disable or revoke this permission at any time in your device's system settings.

## 4. External Links & Third-Party Services
Formascribe includes optional buttons that open external websites or apps in your device's default browser or installed applications, including:
* **Google Images** and **Pinterest** (to search for visual reference ideas based on the active prompt)
* **Ko-fi** (`https://ko-fi.com/formascribe`, if you choose to support development)

When you tap these links, you leave Formascribe and are subject to the privacy policies and terms of those respective third-party platforms.

## 5. Children's Privacy
Formascribe does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

## 6. Changes to This Policy
If future updates introduce new features that affect data handling, this Privacy Policy will be updated at this URL with a revised effective date.
